MAGI privacy policy

MAGI is a private, self-hosted personal assistant. Its only user is its developer, who runs it on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the developer's consent, MAGI reads the developer's own Google Calendar events, Gmail messages and Google Sheets. It archives or bins emails, and adds events to its own calendar, only when the developer confirms.

Where data goes

Data is stored on the developer's own computer. To answer the developer, MAGI sends relevant parts of it to the AI assistant and messaging services the developer has chosen for their own use. It is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's secure keychain.

MAGI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
